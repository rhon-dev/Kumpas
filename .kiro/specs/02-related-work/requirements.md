# Phase 2 — Related Work Grounding

## Objective

Position Kumpas honestly against existing FSL/ASL recognition research, clearly delineating what is novel (corrective feedback) from what is prerequisite (classification).

## Requirements

WHEN the related-work summary is produced,
the system SHALL cite at least the key FSL recognition works (SENYAS, Sign-Bridge, FSL-105 dataset paper) and relevant ASL/ISL feedback systems.

WHEN the novelty statement is written,
the system SHALL explicitly acknowledge what Kumpas does NOT claim novelty on (CNN-LSTM classification of sign landmarks) and what it DOES (real-time corrective feedback on four dimensions, fully on-device).

WHEN positioning against related work,
the system SHALL identify the specific gap Kumpas fills: no existing system provides per-dimension corrective feedback for FSL learners on-device.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Related-work summary covers at minimum: FSL-105 dataset paper, SENYAS, at least one corrective-feedback system from any sign language.
2. Novelty statement is one clear paragraph suitable for a thesis introduction.
3. Citation list is sufficient for a thesis literature review section.
4. Honest about limitations — does not overclaim.
