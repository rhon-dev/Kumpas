# Phase 20 — Post-Defense Maintenance & Hypercare

## Objective

Handle post-defense feedback without ad-hoc processes, and capture future extensions explicitly.

## Requirements

WHEN post-defense feedback is received,
the system SHALL track it in a maintenance backlog with priority and decision (fix / defer / out-of-scope).

WHEN a point release is warranted,
the system SHALL follow the same release checklist as Phase 19 (no shortcuts because "it's just a patch").

WHEN future extensions are identified,
the system SHALL log them in the backlog explicitly marked as out-of-MVP-scope (continuous recognition, iOS, Play Store distribution, etc.) so they don't creep into thesis-defense scope.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Maintenance backlog exists and is usable.
2. Version notes document any post-defense changes.
3. Future extensions are captured but explicitly gated.

## Status

Not applicable until defense is complete.
